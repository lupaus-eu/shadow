## The service and who may use it
- Yearclock is a yearly planning service provided to **business customers only**. The customer is an Organization; its Owners and Admins act for it. `TODO(lawyer)` a clause on authority and on consumers being excluded.
- The service is described by the product as it is when the customer uses it. `TODO(lawyer)` the beta wording: the service is a product beta and may change.

## Price and payment
- The price is EUR 30 per seat per month before tax, billed monthly in advance through Stripe, and every active member (Owners included) occupies one seat. `TODO(lawyer)` confirm the price and any beta price.
- A seat added during the period is charged at once for the days left; a seat removed stays paid until the period ends; nothing is refunded.
- If a renewal payment fails, everything keeps working for seven days, after which the Organization is read-only until an Owner pays.
- The price, the terms, and the end of a beta change only with at least 30 days' notice to the Owners, with a right to object `TODO(lawyer)` the effect of an objection.

## Term and ending
- The subscription continues until cancelled. An Owner may cancel at any time; it then ends at the end of the paid period, with no refund. After that the Organization is read-only, nothing is deleted, and an Owner may subscribe again.
- An Owner may delete the Organization; it can be restored for 30 days, after which its content is purged.
