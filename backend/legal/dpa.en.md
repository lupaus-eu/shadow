## Roles
- For the planning content that the customer's people enter (titles, descriptions, locations, links, names, and anything else), the customer is the controller and Yearclock is the processor.
- For account, sign-in, session, audit, and billing data, Yearclock is a controller; see the privacy notice.

## Annex 1: description of the processing
- **Subject and nature:** hosting and operating a yearly planning service (storing, displaying, exporting, and deleting the customer's planning data).
- **Duration:** while the customer has an Organization, plus the 30-day recovery window and the backup retention period.
- **Types of personal data:** names, email addresses, and roles of the customer's members; and whatever personal data the customer's people choose to put in planning content. The product is not designed for special categories of data `TODO(lawyer)` whether to prohibit them in the terms.
- **Data subjects:** the customer's members, invitees, and people named in planning content.
