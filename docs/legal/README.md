# Legal documents

This directory explains how Shadow's legal texts (terms, data processing
agreement, privacy notice, sub-processors, and the like) are edited, versioned,
published, and accepted. Shadow follows the same practice as Yearclock
(Yearclock ADR-0041): the texts live in the backend in Finnish and English,
are served publicly, and new versions are announced to customers.

No legal texts exist yet; they are specified as part of the SaaS platform work
in `.scratch/`.
