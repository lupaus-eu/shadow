# Separate content purge from financial retention

Organization deletion is recoverable during a retention window, after which
Yearclocks, Events, memberships, and other customer planning content are
permanently purged. Only legally necessary financial records and a minimal
deletion audit record are retained for accounting, disputes, and compliance.
Exact retention durations remain policy-configurable so deletion does not
become justification for indefinite customer-content retention.
